Sample case study

Dental & Healthcare: Local SEO

Improve local search visibility for local customers
IndustryDental & Healthcare
Target marketLocal customers
Primary objectiveImprove local search visibility
Project statusSample case study

The SEO challenge

Local dental practices need to appear when nearby patients search for treatments such as teeth cleaning, dental implants, emergency dentistry and family dentistry. Potential issues to investigate include:

  • Incomplete or inconsistent Google Business Profile information.
  • Service pages not optimized for relevant local keywords.
  • Missing location-specific content and internal links.
  • Inconsistent business name, address and phone number (NAP).
  • Limited review management and local authority signals.
  • Weak tracking of calls, website visits and direction requests.

These are common audit areas, not confirmed problems with a specific clinic.

SEO strategy & work plan

Google Business Profile

  • Review business categories and services.
  • Verify hours, contact details, address and website link.
  • Improve business descriptions and profile content.
  • Set up a compliant review-request process.

Local keyword research

  • Research service-related and location-specific search terms.
  • Map keywords to treatment and location pages.
  • Review local search intent and competitor page structures.

Website & on-page SEO

  • Improve title tags, meta descriptions and heading structure.
  • Optimize service pages for local searches.
  • Strengthen internal linking and mobile usability.
  • Review local business structured data where appropriate.

Tracking & reporting

  • Review Search Console impressions, clicks and queries.
  • Monitor Google Business Profile performance metrics.
  • Track organic traffic and qualified leads where measurable.

Tools used

Google Search ConsoleGoogle Business ProfileGoogle Analytics 4Ahrefs / SemrushRank Math SEO

KPIs to measure

MetricHow to evaluate
Local keyword visibilityTrack target keyword rankings over time
Organic search clicksCompare Search Console reporting periods
Search impressionsMonitor visibility for relevant queries

Project outcome

The intended outcome is a better-optimized local search presence, clearer service-page targeting, improved measurement and more opportunities to reach nearby prospective patients.

Verified results: not provided. No ranking gains, traffic increases or lead growth are claimed without supporting data.

Key takeaway

Local SEO combines accurate business information, useful service pages, relevant local keywords, reputation management and ongoing performance tracking. A consistent strategy helps a dental practice build a stronger foundation for local organic discovery.

Guest Posting & Link Building

Project typeOff-page SEO & guest posting
IndustryDigital marketing / business websites
RoleSEO specialist
Primary goalRelevant backlinks and stronger authority

Project objective

Develop a targeted guest posting strategy that helps websites improve their backlink profile, reach relevant audiences and support long-term organic search visibility.

Challenges identified

  • Finding relevant websites within the target niche.
  • Evaluating publisher quality and topical relevance.
  • Identifying guest posting and contributor opportunities.
  • Creating content that provides value to readers.
  • Maintaining natural, relevant and editorially appropriate backlinks.

Strategy & execution

1. Guest post prospecting

Research sites by niche relevance, audience fit, editorial standards and publishing opportunities.

2. Website quality analysis

Evaluate publishers using SEO metrics, organic visibility, content quality and backlink profile signals.

3. Outreach & communication

Personalized outreach emails, relevant content ideas and communication with editors.

4. Content planning

Useful, original topics aligned with the publisher's audience and the target website's subject.

5. Contextual link placement

Natural links within relevant editorial content where they genuinely help readers, following publisher policies and search engine guidelines.

6. Reporting & monitoring

A record of outreach, responses, approved articles, published URLs and backlink status.
